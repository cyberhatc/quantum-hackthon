# Problem 7: Quantum Portfolio Optimizer

## Problem Statement

Cloud service providers offer multiple services with varying costs, benefits, and risks. Optimizing service allocation under budget constraints is analogous to financial portfolio optimization.

## Challenge

Apply portfolio optimization theory to cloud service allocation, finding the optimal balance of cost, benefit, and risk.

## Requirements

1. **Service Modeling**:
   - Define services with cost, benefit, and risk metrics
   - Model service dependencies and constraints
   - Calculate expected returns

2. **Optimization**:
   - Maximize benefit under budget constraint
   - Minimize risk for given return level
   - Find efficient frontier

3. **Quantum Approach**:
   - Use quantum-inspired optimization
   - Compare with classical greedy/linear programming
   - Demonstrate quantum advantage potential

4. **Visualization**:
   - Efficient frontier plot
   - Risk-return trade-off
   - Allocation comparison

## Input

- List of services with cost, benefit, risk
- Budget constraint
- Risk tolerance level

## Output

- Optimal service allocation
- Efficient frontier
- Risk-return analysis
- Quantum vs classical comparison

## Evaluation Criteria

- Optimization quality
- Constraint handling
- Quantum advantage demonstration
- Visualization clarity
